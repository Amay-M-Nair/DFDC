Generators have trouble with fine, repetitive, high-contrast structures inside the mouth. Common artefacts include teeth merged into a single white block, an implausible number of teeth, missing gaps between teeth, blurred lip edges and inconsistent tongue or inner-mouth shading. Re-enactment methods that drive mouth motion (e.g. Face2Face, NeuralTextures) particularly affect this region. Attention concentrated on the mouth area is consistent with these artefacts.
