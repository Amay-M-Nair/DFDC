A face composited from another source, or synthesised without an explicit 3D model, often has shading that does not match the scene's lighting. Examples are shadows under the nose or cheekbones that point the wrong way, specular highlights on the forehead that do not match the other light cues, and missing self-shadowing. Geometric cues include implausible nose or face asymmetry. Attention on the nose and the main shading planes of the face (cheeks, forehead) can indicate lighting or geometry inconsistencies.
