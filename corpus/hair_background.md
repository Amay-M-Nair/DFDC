Fully synthetic faces (StyleGAN-family) often show artefacts outside the face itself: hair strands that dissolve into the background, earrings or glasses that do not match on both sides, distorted or semantically meaningless backgrounds, and "water-droplet" blobs (StyleGAN1). GAN colour handling can also produce unusual saturation statistics. Attention on the hairline, ears or background is consistent with whole-image synthesis rather than a localised face swap.
