Synthetic and swapped faces often have overly smooth, waxy skin. Pores, fine wrinkles and natural blemishes are missing, while texture statistics differ from those of real skin captured by a camera. Global texture analysis (e.g. Gram-matrix features) separates real from fake faces well and is robust to some post-processing. Attention spread across the large skin areas of the cheeks and forehead is consistent with a texture-statistics cue.
