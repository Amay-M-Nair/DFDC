When a classifier labels an image as real, the class-discriminative heatmap shows which evidence supports authenticity, not where an artefact is. Typical authentic cues are natural skin micro-texture, consistent specular highlights in both eyes, well-defined individual teeth, coherent hair strands and a background with realistic structure. A saliency map shows where the model looked, not why. Explanations should therefore describe the highlighted region and plausible cues without claiming certainty about the underlying cause.
