Real eyes photographed under the same light sources show matching corneal specular highlights: the reflections in both eyes have consistent shape, position and colour. GAN-generated faces often have mismatched highlights, irises of different colour or shape, irregular (non-circular) pupils, or asymmetric eyelids. In video, unnatural blink rates were an early deepfake cue. Strong detector attention on one or both eyes suggests the model is relying on ocular inconsistencies.
